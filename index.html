About

People operations, engineered.

Most HR runs on manual spreadsheets and vendor limitations. I treat those as problems to solve, not constraints to live with.

I work at the intersection of people operations and systems design. Across pan-African institutions, from a continental trade secretariat to a fast-growing energy-technology company, I've run the HR operations that keep organizations moving and built the digital systems that make those operations faster, cleaner, and more accountable.

That combination is the point. Most HR runs on manual spreadsheets and vendor limitations; most systems work ignores how people actually operate. I do both: workforce analytics, HRIS configuration and data governance, performance and leave operations, and the automation, dashboards, and internal tools that replace manual reporting. I've served payroll and HR systems for organizations ranging from a few hundred staff to 700-plus.

I'm currently working toward the SHRM-CP certification, formalizing a practice I've mostly built by doing.
